5Cloudflare tunnel (optional)

A tunnel makes the portal reachable from the internet at your public web name (step 4), through your own Cloudflare account. Create a tunnel in Cloudflare, copy its token (or the whole install command it shows) and paste it here. After the installation you add a few routes in Cloudflare: the installer prints the list. Skip this and the portal works on your local network only.
