    Data analyst onboarding

    Data analyst onboarding is the front door for a new data analyst: what to read first and where every DA Management tool lives.

    Contents
    1. First day
    2. DA Management in short
    3. Weekly rhythm
    4. Going deeper

    Welcome. This wiki is the clean set of notes for running the mission portal. Start here, then open the pages you need.

    First day

    1. Sign in to the portal with your Data Analyst account.
    2. Open Wiki in the side menu (Information). You are reading it.
    3. Open DA Management (Manager tools) for imports, places, accounts and updates.
    4. Read Roles and permissions and Uploads / Importer.

    DA Management in short

    • Uploads — one page for every file the mission imports: the weekly roster, historical Weekly Planning CSVs, people, geo data, Vollzogen, and the other data uploads. Each section has a styled file picker, a check, then Apply as one undoable batch.
    • People — Account Manager, staff accounts, role abilities, and links to web services.
    • Places — close and reopen zones, districts and areas; area mappings; planning questions; delete retired items for good.
    • History — Import history (with Undo) and Updates from GitHub.

    Weekly rhythm

    • Missionaries fill Weekly Planning each week; leaders use Call-ins.
    • Dashboards, Archetypal Health and Presentations sit under Insights.
    • Reminders stay on: the portal keeps Sunday planning reminders even when the browser blocks notifications.
