    Main page

    Main page is the documentation wiki for Weekly Planning 3.0, the Germany Frankfurt Mission portal.

    Welcome

    This wiki explains how to use, manage, and (later) install Weekly Planning 3.0. It is written in plain English for three audiences:

    1. Portal users — missionaries, district and zone leaders, APs, and the mission president
    2. Data analysts / admins — uploads, places, roster, accounts, dashboards, DBeaver, backups, health
    3. Other missions — installing their own copy (installer is planned; see Installer overview)

    Nothing here invents features. Where a fact still needs a person to confirm it on the live system, you will see a needs checking note or a stub notice.

    Did you know…

    • …that Sunday is the reporting-week key — plans lock to an area, a ward/branch, and that Sunday (Friday planning habits welcome; the week still keys off Sunday)?
    • …that hiding a menu button is not security — the database and portal-api decide who may do what?
    • …that a roster upload wins over a hand-made close of a zone, district, or area?
    • …that you can present with two windows of the same browser (audience + /presenter), and chart clicks in one do not sync to the other?
    • …that district council runs smoother when companionships submit Weekly Planning before the meeting — less “we’ll fill it in later”?
    • …that P-day is for laundry and grocery runs, not for rewriting the roster by hand?
