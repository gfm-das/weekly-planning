Remembered area mappings

Map historical labels to areas in the newest accepted roster. Historical labels remain unchanged, and future uploads reuse confirmed mappings. Only new or changed rows are saved.

Each old label stands for one area, and its numbers are never added to another label's. Choose Closed area for an area that no longer exists. Only a Combined companionship (one companionship serving two areas) adds numbers together, and Separate takes them apart again.

New labels come with a suggestion (marked Suggested, with the reason). Check each one: nothing is saved or imported until you press Save.

Close old area is optional and never preselected. It deactivates the active area whose name equals the label, or the label without its zone prefix (for example Frankfurt-Darmstadt S → Darmstadt S). The mapped target area is never deactivated, and area deactivation is not part of import undo.
