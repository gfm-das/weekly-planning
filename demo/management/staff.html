Staff accounts

Sign-ins for the mission president, office staff and Data Analysts who do not serve in an area. Staff accounts do not have a weekly plan. Missionaries get their sign-in from Account Manager.

Add a staff account

  • President: Mission president. Sees and helps manage everything in the mission: Dashboards, Presentations, Call-ins, the calendar and DA Management.
  • Data Analyst: Keeps the mission's data and the portal working: accounts, imports, planning questions and reports. Sees the whole mission, like the President.
  • Office: Edits the mission calendar and records meeting attendance. No Dashboards, Call-ins or DA Management.

An invitation email goes to this address. If the address already has a sign-in without a missionary, that sign-in is used and gets a password email instead.
