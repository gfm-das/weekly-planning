Weekly Planning

Planning questions

Missionaries answer these questions every week in Weekly Planning. Ask only what helps companionships plan and leaders help. Every question adds time to someone's Sunday.

Wording changes show at once on plans that are still drafts. Submitted plans never change. The questions about new members, friends with a baptismal date and high-potential friends are fixed in the portal and cannot be changed here.

Preview the form Change history Delete a retired question for good

Locked questions are read by Dashboards, Call-ins, Presentations or Archetypal Health. You can change their wording, help text, order and number limits. Their key, type, Required setting, answer choices and show/hide rules stay the same, and they cannot be retired. Nothing is ever deleted: a retired question or section leaves the form, and saved answers stay.

A manager can unlock a locked question on its page. Every unlock and lock is in the change history with who did it and why.
