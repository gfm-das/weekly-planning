Account Manager

Filter a group, select all visible missionaries, then send account setup. Missing accounts receive an invite; existing accounts receive a password-reset email. Existing accounts are also linked to the correct roster missionary, and a sign-in whose roster email changed moves to the new address. You see what will happen for each person before anything is sent.
