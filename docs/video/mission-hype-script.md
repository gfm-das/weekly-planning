# Mission HYPE video: script and storyboard (product showcase)

**Purpose:** introduce the portal to the whole mission. The video is a **product showcase**: it shows the portal itself, clean and close, the way a company shows a new app. There is one message: **the portal exists, and it makes your life easier.**
**Length:** about 80 seconds (target 60 to 90). **Format:** 16:9, 1080p, captions burned in; a vertical 9:16 cut of the same scenes for phones.
**Voice:** calm, confident, friendly; one narrator (a missionary's voice, no accent requirement). **Music:** light, modern, steady build, no lyrics, ducked under the voice.
**Data:** every screen is recorded from the portal with **fake data only** (made-up areas, zones and people such as "Area Example", "Elder Sample"). No real names, faces, photos, phone numbers or ward names appear anywhere. Check each frame before release.

## The idea in one line
"The portal is here. Your week just got easier."

Round 13 owner answer 4: the benefit to stress is that the portal exists and makes their life easier, and the video should look like a product showcase. So every scene answers the same question, **"what does this save me?"**, and ends on a short "easier" line. The New Member (NM) benefit stays as **one** of the scenes: the NM questions are no longer something to fill in every week.

## The look (what "product showcase" means here)
- **Screens are the stars.** No street scenes, no paper stacks, no actors. The portal on a laptop frame and on a phone frame, floating on a soft gradient in the mission colours.
- **One feature per scene**, each with a small feature tag at the lower left ("Weekly Planning", "Call-ins", "Calendar", ...) and one benefit line in large type ("Plan together." "See it at once.").
- **Slow, smooth camera**: gentle zoom-ins on the part that matters, a highlighted cursor, a soft glow on the clicked button, a ring around what changes. No shaky screen recording, no fast cuts.
- **Same motion every time**: a scene slides in from the right, the benefit line fades up, the scene dissolves out. A calm rhythm of about 6 to 9 seconds per feature.
- **Consistent frame**: the same device frame, the same type, the same corner for the feature tag. Light theme throughout (one short dark-theme moment in the languages scene is fine).
- **No hype words.** Plain, true statements about what the portal does today.

## How to produce a draft (when `ffmpeg` and a headless browser are available)
1. Start the demo site (task 26, `demo/`, fake data) or a throw-away install loaded with fake data. Never record the live portal. Pages the demo does not include (Weekly Planning, Dashboards, Presentations, the drive) are recorded from a throw-away install.
2. Record each scene as its own clip with a headless browser at 1920x1080, 30 fps, from a scripted walk-through per scene (so it can be redone when the portal changes). Add the device frames, gradient background, cursor glow and feature tags in the edit, not in the portal.
3. Cut and caption with `ffmpeg` (concatenate, burn `.srt`, add the music bed at -18 dB under the voice).
4. Check: no real names, faces, photos or phone numbers; no URL of the live server; no drive ID on screen; the portal shows only fake data.
Nothing in this file needs the video to exist: the script and storyboard are the deliverable for the owner's review.
